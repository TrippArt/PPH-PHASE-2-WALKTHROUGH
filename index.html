How to move

  • Phone or tablet: touch anywhere and push up to walk forward, pull down to back up, slide sideways to turn. A short push walks at the regular pace. Push all the way up and you move twice as fast.
  • Two fingers: slide up to look up, down to look down.
  • Right mouse button (computer): hold it down to draw on the wall the dot is pointing at, and let go to stop. The brush button at the top left picks one of five colours and a size from ½″ to 12″. Marks are temporary until you press Save for the team, which opens a GitHub issue from your own account with the marks filled in; submit it and a bot adds them to the site for everyone within a few minutes. Untick "Markups on the walls" in the menu to see the clean walls.
  • Computer: click the view once. Then the arrow keys or W A S D walk forward, back and step sideways, and moving the mouse looks around. Q and E turn. Hold Shift to go three times faster. Esc frees the mouse pointer, M opens this menu. If the mouse pointer stays visible after the click, hold the button and drag to look.
  • Overview and Plan: drag to turn or pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Working choices in this model
  • Wall names follow PPH Walkthrough V4: M is a mural, B is a band, S numbers are walk stops.
  • Eye height is 5′9″. Room depth uses the direct B2 to M4 reading, 60′7½″.
  • The walking view uses a 1× phone camera lens (24 mm equivalent): across the width of the screen you see what a 1× photo taken from the same spot shows, held the same way. 0.5× matches the ultra-wide camera (13 mm). There is no haze, so far walls are as clear as near ones.
  • Keyboard walking is 4½′ a second, an ordinary walking pace, and eases in and out like a person starting and stopping. Shift is three times that.
  • M4 is 49′ and the B4 band runs 146′ past it, per the October measurements. V4 carried 36′ and 159′. B4 is the Mirror Band, the band above the mirrors (Tripp, 11 October). B5 is a different band, on the box face over M6.
  • The bottom trim is drawn 8″ high along the foot of every wall, Tripp's working value from his note on the 7 October panorama. (The only taped trim figure is 4″, in the Zone 4 hallway; the Viper photo reads about 7″.) Designs work to true measurements and ratios, so an inch either way is fine. The trim is its own layer laid over the wall photos and designs, so a photo that already shows the trim and one that does not end up the same. It stops at every doorway, together with the door casing, and runs under the windows.
  • The B4 mirrors: Michael bought 48′ of mirror (11 October), 8′ tall, standing on the top edge of the bottom trim, so 8″ to 8′8″ off the floor. The model starts them 9′ past the end of M4 (Tripp's working value, 8′ to 10′) and runs the 48′ from there, so they end 57′ past M4 with 89′ of plain wall to the M7 corner. Where the 48′ sits along the wall is still open. From the mirror tops to the ceiling is about 4′11″ of band. The mirrors are their own layer; they reflect the room live.
  • Wall 4 is worked in sections the size of M4: 49′ × 13′7″ (5880 × 1630 px). Section 1 is M4. Sections 2, 3 and 4 (B4-1, B4-2, B4-3) repeat that size along the B4 Mirror Band, so the M4 mural is never cut and every section shares one canvas. Section 4 reaches the M7 corner after 48′, so the last 1′ of its canvas is past the corner. A thin pink dotted line marks each split and a small pink tag sits at the top right of each section.
  • M7 (the Viper) is 58′8″ and M8 (the Tiger) is 71′11″, from V4. The M7 door is 10′ wide and 9′ high, at the far right end of the wall.
  • The box is drawn about 8′ longer than the females partition, which makes it about 43′5″ long. The east wall between the box and M8, about 40′7″, is not measured and has no name yet.
  • M6 is 35′5″ and placed at the Wall 1 end of the box face. Its door is not placed.
  • The females room partition is planned. Its length, depth (11′) and height (10′) are placeholders.
  • M8 is set in line with the end of M7, about 2′ in from the B2 line.
  • B3 openings are placed by working back from the M4 corner. B2 opening top is 6′7½″ with a 4″ casing until scaled from the photo.
  • Four columns, each 22½″ across (1.8 × the earlier 12½″, Tripp, 12 October) and 19′2″ from M4, in one line down the hall. Column 1 is assumed on the Wall 1 line, 39′1″ from B3, and the others repeat that spacing.
  • The B4 mirrors reflect the room for real: every frame the model is drawn once more from a camera mirrored through the glass, so you see the far walls, the ceiling and the floor behind you as a mirror would show them (lettering reads backwards, as it should). You appear in them as a simple blocky figure at the 5′9″ eye line, about 6′2″ tall. Its arms and legs hold one of three positions and switch only while you are moving, with the keys or with a finger. The face is avatar/head.png: Michael's head, hat and beard as a transparent cutout, hung on the neck as a thin plate in front of the block head so the mirror sees it from any angle (a square picture would be painted onto the front of the block instead). Overview and Plan show the figure standing where you are. While you walk, your own forearms hang under your shoulders just inside the bottom corners of the screen and swing with your steps; look down a little and you see more of them, then your body and feet, as in a game. The tee is white with PUMP IT! on the chest.
  • Wall markups: anyone can draw on a wall with the right mouse button. Saved marks live in markups.json in the repository, written by a bot from the GitHub issue the Save button opens, with the author's GitHub name. The file is capped at 200 KB; when a save would push it past that, every earlier mark is erased and the site starts again with that save.
  • Wall photos are stretched to fit the measured walls, with doors and windows pinned to their measured positions. The Viper (M7), the Tiger (M8), B3, B2 with its bulkhead and Wall 1 carry photos so far. The M8 photo is stretched to the V4 width. Its three doors (about 28″ wide, 6′8″ to 6′10″ high) and the 5′ × 8′ opening at its right end are cut out of the wall where the photo shows them, so the trim and the designs stop there. The locker-room double doors at its far left, their left edge on the Viper corner (Tripp, 12 October), are cut as a standard 6′ × 6′8″ pair; they are not in the photo and their size is provisional. The M8 windows are not cut.
  • Designs are Tripp's Photoshop canvas exports from 11 October, laid over the photos on B2, the B2 bulkhead, B3, the Viper and the Tiger. M4 and B4 carry Tripp's fixed three-section design from 12 October, cut at the 49′ line (M4 is Section 1, B4 runs Sections 2 to 4 to the Viper corner), over his background wall colour, with the bottom trim and the mirrors in front. Phones and tablets load 4096 px copies; computers load the full exports.
  • The M4 mural photo is laid on unstretched, floor to ceiling from the B3 corner. At that size it is about 50′6″ long, so its last 1′6″ of foliage and blue wall runs past the 49′ mark onto B4.
  • The Wall 1 doorway is new and comes from the photo: about 3′1½″ wide and 6′9″ high, with its near edge about 7½″ from the bulkhead. The photo is pinned to the bulkhead edge and underside, and the spare length is taken up in the blank wall left of the doorway.
  • On B2 the photo stops about 2′ short of the Wall 1 corner, so the curved end is stretched to reach it. B3 Window 1 is squeezed to its measured 7′; in the photo it looks the same size as Window 2.
  • The eye-level line is drawn at 5′9″ on every wall. It stays at that height however near you stand. The view circle marks what falls inside a 60° cone of vision around the point you are looking at, so it follows you when you look up or down and shrinks as you walk closer. Reset brings your view back to level.
  • Measure puts a tape on the 5′9″ eye line of every wall. It counts in feet from the wall's left end as you face it, with a number every 5′, and each end carries the wall's full width and height. The tag above the dot gives the wall you are looking at, its width and height, and how far the dot is from the left and right ends. M4 and B4 are one wall, so their tape runs 0 to 195′ without a break. Heights are floor to ceiling: 12′11″ under the low ceiling, 13′7″ elsewhere.
  • The high ceiling carries the black 2′ × 2′ tile ceiling on one continuous grid starting at the B2 | B3 corner. Where the real grid starts is not measured. The low ceiling by B2 stays white, as in the B3 photo. Lights are not modeled.
  • Wall thickness is 6″ for looks only.
  • Not in this model: the lobby side of B2 (M2), other equipment, the hallway to Zone 4. The three monolifts and two racks are provisional foreground placeholders; see their menu controls.
